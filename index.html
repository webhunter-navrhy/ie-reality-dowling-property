Location, Location, Location.

Welcome to No.74 Shrewsbury Park. Tucked away in this sleepy cul-de-sac in this exclusive gated development, is where you'll find this wonderful, 3 bedroom semi detached home for sale. Located in one of Ireland's most sought after addresses in the heart of Ballsbridge, just off the Merrion Road, with an array of 1st class amenities all within walking distance of your front door.

Built by the Cosgrave Brothers to the highest standards, this fine home is being sold for the very 1st time since it was constructed and the sale is "Chain Free" and ready to go. The accommodation extends to c.93 sq.mt. and features 2 reception rooms, guest w.c., utility, en-suite, upgraded gas heating and a wonderful sitting room, complete with bay window.

The gardens are a real highlight and the rear enjoys complete privacy and overlooks Sydney Parade Cricket Grounds and is very generous in size. The front garden is landscaped and has off-st-parking on a cobble lock driveway.

The location is simply unbeatable with some of Dublin's top venues within walking distance of your front doorstep, including the Aviva Stadium, the R.D.S and Donnybrook Stadium. St. Vincent's Hospital is close by along with the Merrion Shopping Centre and Blackrock. You have the choice of 2 Dart stations close by and Dublin's Stephen's Green is also within walking distance.

Not only is this fine home located in this highly sought after development, but its located in the most preferred position within Shrewsbury Park, in this quiet cul-de-sac. With its "chain free" status and unrivalled location, we must now advise on early viewing as we are predicting strong interest. A pure gem!!

Accommodation

The accommodation, which is bright and well laid out, consists of an entrance hallway, guest w.c., utility/cloakroom, sitting room, kitchen and dining room. Upstairs are 3 bedrooms ( 2 Double/ 1Single ) with master en-suite and family bathroom.

An array of 1st class amenities are located on your doorstep including the R.D.S., Aviva Stadium, Baggot St, Sandymount Sea Front, along with St. Vincent's Hospital and Merrion Road Shopping Centre all within walking distance. The location simply does not get better.
